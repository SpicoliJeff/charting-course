Hybrid Chord Logic
Diatonic Base: Chords begin on the selected scale degree, using the scale’s notes as the foundation.
Chromatic Extensions: If the chord type requires tones outside the scale (maj7, dom7, aug, dim7, add9, etc.), those tones are added chromatically.
Alteration Handling: Chromatic tones are labeled with interval notation (♭2, ♯4, ♭6, etc.) and highlighted as altered tones.
Functional Behavior: Triads and sevenths follow diatonic function; extended and altered chords follow chromatic function.
Prosody Integration: Chord tones still receive solfege + feel lines based on the underlying scale, even when chromatic tones appear.
Interval Labels: Every note shows its interval from the scale root: scale tones as diatonic degrees, chromatic tones as chromatic labels.
